# Code of Conduct

RishDesk is a friendly place to build and use a launcher. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and sharing other people's private information.
- Keep content free of sexual or violent material.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and issues, and may block people who repeatedly break these rules.

To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile [@engineerharish05](https://github.com/engineerharish05) and ask for a private channel. Reports are handled privately.

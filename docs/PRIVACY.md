# RishDesk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

RishDesk is a free, open-source Android launcher made by Rish Innovations. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

**Data collection.** RishDesk does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

**What stays on your device.** Your launcher settings (layout, dock, favourites, hidden apps, folders, accent colour and similar) and the wallpaper you pick are stored locally in the app's private storage. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

**Permissions.**
- *Network state / Bluetooth state:* used only to show Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and battery status in the launcher status bar.
- *Request delete packages:* used only when you choose Uninstall on an app; Android asks you to confirm.
- *Modify system settings (optional):* used only if you turn on "Open apps in landscape"; you can leave it off.
- *Widgets:* when you add a widget, Android may ask for that widget's own permissions. The widget belongs to its app and follows that app's privacy policy.

**Children.** RishDesk does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with an updated date.

**Contact.** Rish Innovations — open an issue at https://github.com/engineerharish05/Rish-Desk/issues or contact the maintainer through GitHub.

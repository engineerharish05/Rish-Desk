Your phone, laid out like a desktop.

RishDesk is a landscape-first launcher with a frosted-glass home screen, a dock, a fast app drawer and a status bar. It works with gesture and 3-button navigation, and it never asks for the internet.

Built for the wide screen

Everything is arranged for a phone held sideways, with the speed and tidiness of a desktop.

Home

A real desktop grid

A 5 × 4 grid by default. Drag shortcuts around, group them in folders, rename them and resize widgets to the limits each widget allows.

Look

Frosted glass

Translucent panels with bright edges, your choice of accent colour and an opacity slider so the glass looks the way you like.

Drawer

Find any app fast

Search by app name, mark favorites, hide apps you do not want to see and bring them back whenever you like.

Dock

Dock and shortcuts

Pin your main apps, jump to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Display and Storage, and open a USB drive from the OTG tile when one is plugged in.

Wallpaper

Frame it with your fingers

Pick a photo, drag and pinch to frame it exactly, then tap Set. Dim and blur are one tap away.

Keyboard

Made for keyboards too

With a keyboard attached, Ctrl, Alt or Meta plus D opens the drawer, comma opens settings, H goes home and 1 to 9 launches your dock apps.

Install in four steps

RishDesk is not on the Play Store, so you install it from the APK file.

  1. Download the APKTap Download APK above. If your browser warns that this type of file can harm your device, choose Download anyway.
  2. Allow the installOpen the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager for this one time.
  3. Press HomeAndroid asks which Home app to use. Choose RishDesk.
  4. Pick AlwaysChoose Always to keep RishDesk as your launcher.

Questions

Android says the file may be unsafe. Is it?

Android shows that notice for every app installed outside the Play Store. The full source code is public on GitHub, and every release has a checksum file so you can confirm your download matches the one that was published.

Does it work with gesture navigation and 3-button navigation?

Yes, both. Android draws its own navigation controls, and RishDesk lays itself out around them.

How do I go back to my old launcher?

Open Android Settings, then Apps, then Default apps, then Home app, and choose your previous launcher. The Exit tile in the dock opens the same chooser.

Can it turn every app to landscape?

No. Android does not let a launcher force an app that only supports portrait into landscape. RishDesk has an optional landscape setting that helps apps able to rotate. It also cannot show a taskbar of running apps.

Which phones has it been tested on?

Mainly a Realme C61 running Android 16. If something looks wrong on your phone, please report it on GitHub with your phone model and Android version.

What does RishDesk do with my data?

Nothing leaves your phone. The app has no internet permission, no accounts and no analytics. Read the full privacy policy.
